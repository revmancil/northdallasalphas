Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc.

National Programs

Xi Tau Lambda Chapter executes Alpha Phi Alpha's four cornerstone national programs, bringing the fraternity's commitment to scholarship, service, and civic engagement directly to North Dallas County.

Our Foundation

Programs Built on a Century of Service

Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc. was founded in 1906 at Cornell University as the first intercollegiate Greek-letter fraternity established for African American men. From its earliest days, the fraternity understood that brotherhood without service is incomplete.

The national programs carried out by Xi Tau Lambda Chapter are not checkbox initiatives — they are the expression of a living commitment to the communities we serve. Every voter registered, every student mentored, every young man taught to own his future, every formerly incarcerated man given a second chance — this is the work.

Programs in Action

What We Do in North Dallas

Each program targets a distinct need in our community — from the ballot box to the classroom, from the youth center to the reentry office.

A Voteless People Is a Hopeless People

Our voter engagement initiative reaches thousands of North Dallas residents through registration drives, civic education workshops, and get-out-the-vote mobilization during every major election cycle. Rooted in Alpha's Civil Rights legacy, this program honors those who fought and died for the right to vote.

Learn More

Go-to-High School, Go-to-College

Xi Tau Lambda brothers partner with North Dallas school districts to deliver college readiness workshops, mentorship, financial aid guidance, and campus visits — opening the doors of higher education for young men and women who might not otherwise see it as an option.

Learn More

Project Alpha

Developed in partnership with the March of Dimes, Project Alpha brings honest, brother-led conversations about health, responsibility, peer pressure, and character to young men in North Dallas middle and high schools. Every session is free and facilitated by active chapter brothers.

Learn More

Brother's Keeper

Our reentry mentorship initiative provides one-on-one support, workforce development workshops, financial literacy education, and community connections to men navigating life after incarceration. Xi Tau Lambda believes in the capacity of every man to rebuild and contribute.
