Leadership Development Institute

National Program

Developing Leaders Who Serve

The Leadership Development Institute (LDI) is Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity's structured leadership education program, designed to develop the intellectual, organizational, and service capacity of fraternity members and community leaders alike. LDI equips participants with the tools to lead effectively in their chapters, their professions, and their communities — at the local, regional, and national level.

Rooted in Alpha's foundational belief that scholarship and service must be matched by capable, principled leadership, LDI addresses a simple truth: good intentions are not enough. The men who change communities are those who couple their commitment to service with the skills to organize, communicate, advocate, and execute. LDI is where that transformation happens.

Xi Tau Lambda Chapter participates in and promotes LDI programming at the district and national level, and delivers chapter-level leadership development sessions throughout the year. Our brothers invest in LDI because we believe the quality of our leadership determines the impact of our service — and North Dallas County deserves our very best.

Program Structure

Three Levels of Leadership

LDI is structured as a progressive curriculum — each level builds on the last, developing leaders from chapter service through national engagement.

I

Chapter Leadership

Foundational leadership skills for chapter officers and active brothers — meeting facilitation, parliamentary procedure, project management, and chapter governance.

II

District Leadership

Expanded leadership competencies for brothers serving at the district level — coalition building, community advocacy, strategic planning, and multi-chapter coordination.

III

National Leadership

Advanced leadership development for brothers pursuing regional and national roles — organizational leadership, policy development, public representation, and legacy building.

Core Competencies

What LDI Develops

Every LDI session addresses the competencies that define effective, principled leadership in fraternity, profession, and community.

Communication & Advocacy

Public speaking, persuasive communication, media engagement, and the ability to articulate a vision and move people toward a shared goal.

Strategic Planning

Goal setting, program design, budget management, and the operational skills needed to move from vision to execution with measurable results.

Ethics & Integrity

The values foundation of Alpha leadership — accountability, transparency, fairness, and the courage to lead with integrity when it is difficult.

Coalition Building

Building partnerships across organizations, sectors, and communities — the relational skills that multiply impact and sustain long-term change.

Xi Tau Lambda Chapter

LDI in North Dallas

Xi Tau Lambda Chapter integrates LDI principles into every aspect of chapter life. From officer training at the start of each year to leadership workshops open to the broader North Dallas community, we believe developing capable leaders is itself an act of community service.

Our chapter actively encourages brothers to attend district-level LDI programming through the Texas Council of Alpha Chapters (TCAC) and to pursue national LDI certifications that strengthen their capacity to lead both inside and outside the fraternity.

The leadership skills developed through LDI show up in every program we run — from how we plan and execute voter registration drives, to how we structure the Alpha Legacy Group, to how we show up as partners and advocates in the North Dallas community.

LDI Certification

Brothers who complete LDI programming earn national recognition from Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc. — a credential that reflects demonstrated leadership competency at the chapter, district, or national level.

TCAC District Programming

Xi Tau Lambda participates in Texas Council of Alpha Chapters district conventions and leadership events, where LDI sessions bring together brothers from across the state for shared leadership development.

Develop Your Leadership

Ready to Lead at a Higher Level?

Whether you are an active brother seeking LDI certification or a community leader looking to strengthen your impact, Xi Tau Lambda Chapter wants to connect you with the resources and brotherhood that make great leaders.
